GAME MASTER'S KEEP — PHASE 3.21
THE DICE CHAMBER

This update builds on Phase 3.20 and upgrades the existing Game Tools dice roller.

WHAT'S NEW
- Replaces the plain dice form with an atmospheric Dice Chamber.
- Adds a central animated die with a rolling reveal.
- Adds D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20, and D100 selection.
- Supports 1–20 dice per roll.
- Adds a modifier from -99 to +99.
- Shows the total, subtotal, individual dice, and modifier breakdown.
- Highlights a single-die natural 20 and natural 1 on a D20.
- Keeps the eight most recent rolls in a temporary on-screen history.
- Adds a Clear History control.
- Adapts the Dice Chamber to the Fantasy, Starfleet, and Galactic Adventures visual themes.
- Honors reduced-motion accessibility preferences.

NOTES
- Dice are rolled locally in the browser; no subscription, server, or external service is used.
- Roll history is temporary and is cleared when the page is refreshed.
- The dice roller does not modify campaign or character data.
- The existing Game Master's Keep features and local data architecture are retained.
