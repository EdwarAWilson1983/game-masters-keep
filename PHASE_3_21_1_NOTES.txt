PHASE 3.21.1 — DICE CHAMBER VISUAL REFINEMENT

This refinement replaces the generic flat six-sided badge with a custom faceted polyhedral die illustration. The die now has a sculpted silhouette, individually shaded triangular faces, inset center face, engraved-style numerals, metallic edge highlights, and universe-specific face colors for The Chronicles of Puma, Starfleet Command, and Galactic Adventures.

The existing roll animation, result reveal, critical/fumble effects, dice controls, modifiers, breakdown, and history remain in place. No external images, libraries, services, or subscriptions are required.
