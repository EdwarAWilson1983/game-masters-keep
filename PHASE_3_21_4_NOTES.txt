GAME MASTER'S KEEP — PHASE 3.21.4
Dice Chamber: Material realism and visible roll result

Changes:
- Refined the 3D D20 canvas rendering with studio-style directional lighting, richer face gradients, recessed inner bevels, restrained specular reflections, darker edge depth, and engraved-style numerals.
- Reduced the bright cartoon-like facet treatment in favor of more natural material shading across the four existing finishes.
- Added a prominent result medallion directly over the die. It reveals the final roll total after the tumble, while the existing result panel below continues to show the same total and breakdown.
- Natural 20 and natural 1 retain their special highlighting.
- Preserved the existing dice controls, calculations, history, and universe themes.

Implementation note:
The D20 remains a locally rendered geometric canvas model; this is a material and presentation refinement, not a photorealistic ray-traced 3D renderer. No external libraries, CDN dependencies, server, or subscription were added.
